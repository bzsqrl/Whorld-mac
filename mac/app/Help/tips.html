Tips and Gestures

The shortcuts and gestures that are easy to miss. The full story is in Whorld Help.

Over the picture

Do thisTo
Two-finger swipe up or downZoom
A key and ⇧ and swipeMove that key's control faster (⌥ or ⌘: finer)
A key and ↑ ↓Step that key's control, as a number box's arrows (with ⇧, ⌥, ⌘; ⇧⌘ to an end)
Hold a letter and swipeChange that key's control (View > Key Map lists them)
` (left of 1)Show or hide the whole Key Map drawn on a keyboard; swipes still work through it
Press a Key Map key, without swipingSee its control and value in the Perform Bar first
Two-finger tap (or right-click)Put Zoom back; with a letter held, that key's control
Hold the mouse button and moveMove the rings' center (the origin)
Double-clickDetach the picture into its own window, or dock it again
⌘← ⌘→Previous and next snapshot, with snapshots loaded

The Perform Bar

Do thisTo
Click the frame rateSee the rest of the figures: missed frames, draw time, rings, renderer
Click the playlist place (12 / 86)Left number: the previous patch; right number: the next
← or → with the pointer on the playlist placeThe previous or next patch
Right-click PlayPick how long each patch plays
Click the tempo beside ModReset to 1.1: the nearest beat becomes bar 1, beat 1
Option-click the full-screen or Detach buttonGo full screen, or detach, on another display

Sliders and number boxes

Do thisTo
Right-clickPut it back to its value when the patch was loaded or saved
Rest the pointer and swipe with two fingersMove it: sideways on a slider, up or down on a number box
Hold ⌘, ⌥ or ⇧ while swipingGo finer (⌘ finest, ⌥ fine) or coarser (⇧)
Hover over a control's nameSee what it does, and its Key Map key

Panels

Do thisTo
Double-click a panel's title barFloat it in its own window, or dock it again
Drag a title bar onto another panelPlace it beside that panel, or stack them as tabs
Drop a panel in the thin strip along an area's edgeMake it span the whole area
⌥⌘ and an arrow, pointer over a panelMove it a step; with ⇧, all the way
Click the ⓘ by a panel's title (or ⋯ > About)See what the panel is for (the ⓘs show while tips on hover are on)
Drag playlist rowsReorder the playlist
Click a patch in View > Patches (⌥⌘6), then ↑ ↓Play each patch in turn
Drag from Patches into the PlaylistAdd patches to the playlist
Right-click a dimmed playlist patchLocate a missing patch (and others in the same folder)
